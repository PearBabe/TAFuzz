The local changes repair and test the backward symbolic minimum-cost subsystem
described by Parrot and Lime, *Backward Symbolic Optimal Reachability in
Weighted Timed Automata* (FORMATS 2020). They do not claim to repair unrelated
Roméo algorithms or to reproduce the unpublished 2020 source revision.
